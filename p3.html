🔋 What is Battery Swapping?
Swapping is a method of replacing a discharged battery in an electric vehicle (EV) with a fully charged one in just a few minutes, instead of waiting for it to recharge.

How It Works
1. You drive your EV to a battery swap station.

2. The station removes your empty battery.

3. It replaces it with a fully charged battery.

4. You’re back on the road in less than 5 minutes.

🔋 What are the benefits if it you ask...
See for yourself.

Benefits of Battery Swapping
1. Speed: Faster than traditional charging

2. Convenience: Less downtime for drivers

3. Scalability: Suitable for fleet services and urban mobility

4. Battery Lifecycle Management: Centralized battery health monitoring
